Write like a journal.

Tell it what you did and what you want to do. It turns your words into sessions on your calendar, splits big plans into small ones, and suggests when to do each one.

01 · Journal to calendar

Write it once. It's on your calendar.

"Gym twice a week at 6 pm" or "thermo revision tomorrow at 10". Clear plans go straight onto your calendar, and "I finished the lab report" ticks the task off. Vague plans ask first.

02 · Big plans, small sessions

Breaks a big plan into chunks.

"4 cycles in the next 4 days, about an hour each" becomes one session per cycle, in order, cut to your session length with breaks. Link it to an exam and it adds a revision session the evening before.

03 · Suggested times

Picks a time, and learns yours.

Every task gets a suggested time inside your working hours, around college and your days off. At first the AI picks most of it from what you write. As you finish sessions, your own habits take over.

04 · Habits, exams, reminders

Keeps the rest of your life in it too.

Streaks for habits like the gym, countdowns to exams, and birthdays that remind you on the day. It works offline on your phone and laptop and syncs between them.
